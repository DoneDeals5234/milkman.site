📦 FAQs & Refund Policy

When will my morning milk order arrive?

Orders placed before 10:00 PM are freshly bottled and dispatched by 5:00 AM for doorstep delivery before 7:00 AM every morning.

How do cancellations and refunds work?

You can cancel any PENDING order directly from this page with 1 click. Instant 100% refund will be processed back to your original payment UPI or bank source within 2-4 hours.

What if the delivery packet is damaged?

Our milk pouches are tested under rigorous food-grade conditions. If any seal is compromised, click "Chat with Support" or message our helpline at +91 75089 90616 for an instant free replacement.
